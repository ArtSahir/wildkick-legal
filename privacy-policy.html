1. Who we are

The Game is developed and published by CONPLY, an independent game developer. CONPLY decides why and how the information described here is processed and is the contact point for every privacy question. You can reach us at userconplay@gmail.com.

2. The short version

  • Your player name, progress, coins, KIX, items and settings are kept only on your device. We cannot see them.
  • The Game sends play statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase. They never contain the name you type in the Game.
  • The Game is free and shows rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one. The ads come from Google AdMob and Unity Ads, which may use your device’s advertising ID.
  • Where it is required, you are asked for consent before personalized ads are shown, and you can change your answer at any time in Settings → Account & Help → Privacy settings.
  • We never ask for your contacts, photos, camera, microphone or precise location, and we do not sell your personal information.

3. Information kept only on your device

The following is saved in the Game’s private storage on your device. It is not sent to us or to anyone else. The save file is signed, so a copy changed outside the Game is refused.

InformationWhy it is kept
Player name (typed by you, optional) and chosen avatarShown on your player card inside the Game.
Game progress: stages cleared, stars, best scores and records, achievements, journey position, level and experienceSo you can continue where you left off.
Coins, KIX and items you have earned, bought in the store with them, and equippedSo the store and your character work.
Settings: language, volumes, vibration, controls, ball path, camera, graphics, frame rate, interface size and the daily reminderSo the Game behaves the way you set it.
Daily rewards: daily gift and daily challenge stateSo each daily reward is given once.
Your saved copy, made with Save nowSo you can restore your progress on the same device.
Your consent choices for ads, stored by Google’s consent toolSo you are not asked again on every launch.

You can erase all of it at any time: see Your Data & Deletion.

4. Information collected automatically

When you play while connected to the internet, the services below receive information from your device. They are provided by Google LLC and Unity Technologies, who process it as described here and in their own privacy policies (see section 12). All of it travels encrypted.

ServiceInformationPurpose
Google Analytics for Firebase A random app-instance identifier; gameplay events (a stage started or finished with its result, score and stars, screens opened, tutorial steps, level ups, achievements unlocked, coins and KIX earned and spent, daily rewards claimed, rewarded ads watched or not available); device model, operating system version, language, app version and session times; approximate location (country or region) derived from a masked IP address; how the Game was installed (for example from Google Play); your device’s advertising ID where available. Understanding how the Game is played, balancing its difficulty, measuring which features work, and finding problems.
Firebase Crashlytics Crash and error reports: the technical stack trace, device model, operating system version, app version, time of the crash and the device’s state at that moment (such as free memory and storage), a random installation identifier, the stage being played and the screens opened just before the crash, and a fingerprint of the certificate the app was signed with. Finding and fixing crashes, and recognising modified copies of the Game.
Google AdMob (Google Mobile Ads SDK and User Messaging Platform) and Unity Ads (through AdMob mediation) Your device’s advertising ID and app set ID and other identifiers used by the ad services; IP address and the approximate location estimated from it; device and app information (model, operating system, language, screen, app version) and diagnostics about the ad services’ performance; ad interactions (ads requested, shown, watched to the end, tapped); on supported Android versions, the Android Privacy Sandbox advertising APIs. Showing rewarded ads, measuring them, preventing fraud, and personalizing ads where you allow it.
Not linked to you by name. None of these services receives the player name you type in the Game, and we do not combine their information with anything that identifies you.

5. Information we do not collect

  • No account, e-mail address, phone number or password: the Game has no sign-in.
  • No contacts, photos, videos, files, camera, microphone or precise (GPS) location.
  • No payment information: the Game sells nothing for real money. Coins and KIX are earned by playing.
  • No messages: the Game has no chat.

7. How the information is used

  • To run the Game and keep your progress on your device.
  • To understand how the Game is played and improve its stages, difficulty and features.
  • To detect and fix crashes and errors.
  • To show the optional rewarded ads, give you their reward, measure them and prevent ad fraud.
  • To protect the Game against modified copies and cheating.
  • To answer you when you write to us.

We do not use the information to identify you, and we do not sell it.

8. Advertising and your choices

The Game shows only rewarded ads, and only when you tap to watch one: for example to get another ball when you run out, or to receive a daily gift. No ad interrupts a round.

Consent

  • Where it is required, a consent message from Google appears before personalized ads are shown.
  • In some places, a message also lets you opt out of personalized advertising.
  • You can review or change your choice at any time in Settings → Account & Help → Privacy settings.

On your device

You can reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings: Settings → Privacy → Ads on recent Android versions, or Settings → Google → Ads on some devices. After a deletion, apps receive no advertising ID and ads become less relevant. Read how Google uses information for advertising.

Ad partners

  • Google AdMob: read how Google uses information from apps that use its services.
  • Unity Ads: read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

9. Analytics and crash reports

Google Analytics for Firebase and Firebase Crashlytics are provided by Google LLC. The information they receive is listed in section 4. We use it only to understand and improve the Game and to fix it. Read Privacy and Security in Firebase.

10. Notifications

The Game can remind you once a day to come back and play. The reminder is off by default. The system permission to show notifications is requested only when you turn the reminder on in Settings. The reminder is scheduled on your device: no notification server is used and no information is sent for it. You can turn it off in the Game or in your device settings at any time.

11. Google Play Games

When achievements and leaderboards through Google Play Games are available in your version of the Game and you choose to use them, Google Play Games shares your Play Games profile (gamer name, avatar and player identifier) with the Game and keeps your achievements and leaderboard scores. This is governed by the Google Privacy Policy, and you can manage it in the Google Play Games app.

13. How long information is kept

  • On your device: until you delete it in the Game, clear the app’s storage, or uninstall the Game.
  • Google Analytics for Firebase: event data for at most 14 months, then deleted automatically.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports and their identifiers for 90 days.
  • Google AdMob and Unity Ads: as described in their own privacy policies.
  • E-mails you send us: as long as needed to answer you, then deleted.

14. Security

The Game communicates only over encrypted connections (unencrypted traffic is blocked), signs your save file against tampering, and collects as little as it can. No method of storage or transmission is completely secure, but we work to protect the information described here.

15. Your rights

You can:

  • ask what personal information is processed and receive a copy of it;
  • ask for it to be corrected or deleted;
  • object to its use;
  • withdraw your consent at any time, in Privacy settings inside the Game;
  • opt out of personalized ads.

The information on your device is under your control, and you can delete it yourself (see Your Data & Deletion). For anything else, write to userconplay@gmail.com. We answer within 30 days.

16. Children

The Game is made for a general audience of players aged 13 and over and is not directed to children under 13. We do not knowingly collect personal information from children under 13. If you believe a child has given us personal information, contact us and we will delete it.

17. Changes to this policy

We may update this policy when the Game changes. The new version is published on this page with a new effective date, and important changes are also announced in the Game or on its Google Play page.
