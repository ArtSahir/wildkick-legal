WildKick - Football Challenges is a football skills game for Android: twenty challenges and 350 stages, from penalties and free kicks to keep-ups, targets and dribbling, played one ball at a time.

These documents explain what information the Game uses, the rules for playing it, how to delete your data, and who owns it. They apply to the Android app published on Google Play by CONPLY. If anything is unclear, write to userconplay@gmail.com. We answer every request within 30 days.

The short version

  • No account, no sign-in. Your name, progress, coins, KIX, items and settings stay on your device.
  • The Game sends play statistics and crash reports to Google Firebase, without your name, to improve and fix it.
  • It is free, with rewarded ads only when you choose to watch one, served by Google AdMob and Unity Ads, and a consent message where one is required.
  • Coins and KIX are game currency with no real-world value. The Game sells nothing for real money.
  • You can delete everything at any time from Settings → Account & Help → Delete account.
