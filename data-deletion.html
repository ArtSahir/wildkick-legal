WildKick - Football Challenges has no accounts: there is nothing to sign up for and nothing to close. Everything that is yours (name, progress, coins, KIX, items, settings) is kept on your device, and you can delete it yourself at any moment. This page also explains what happens to the technical data held by Google and Unity, and how to ask us for help.

Quickest way: in the Game, open Settings → Account & Help → Delete account and confirm with Delete.

1. Delete everything in the Game

  1. Open WildKick and go to Settings.
  2. Open the Account & Help tab.
  3. Tap Delete account.
  4. Read the warning and tap Delete to confirm.

The deletion happens at once, on your device, and cannot be undone. The Game then starts again as it does for a new player.

3. Other ways on your device

  • Clear the app’s storage: open your phone’s Settings → Apps → WildKick → Storage and tap Clear storage. This erases everything the Game keeps, including your consent choice.
  • Uninstall the Game: Android removes all of its data from the device with it.

The names of these menus can differ slightly between phone makers.

4. Data held by Google and Unity

The technical data described in our Privacy Policy is stored by Google and Unity under random identifiers, not under your name, so neither we nor they can find it from your name or e-mail address. It is removed as follows:

  • Google Analytics for Firebase: play statistics are deleted automatically after at most 14 months.
  • Firebase Crashlytics: crash reports are deleted automatically after 90 days.
  • Google AdMob and Unity Ads: under their own policies. To cut the link with past ad data, reset or delete your advertising ID in your device settings (Settings → Privacy → Ads, or Settings → Google → Ads on some devices). To stop personalized ads, change your choice in the Game under Settings → Account & Help → Privacy settings.

You can also use Google’s and Unity’s own privacy tools: read the Google Privacy Policy and read the Unity Game Player and App User Privacy Policy.

5. Ask us by e-mail

For any deletion or privacy question, write to userconplay@gmail.com with the subject “Delete my data — WildKick”.

  • We answer within 30 days and tell you exactly what we did.
  • We delete our correspondence with you, and any other information we hold that can be linked to you.
  • We cannot delete progress on your device for you: we never receive it. Use section 1 instead.

6. Common questions

Can I get my progress back after deleting it?

No. Deletion is permanent, and the copy made with Save now is deleted too.

Will my coins or KIX be refunded?

No. Coins and KIX are game currency with no monetary value (see the Terms of Use).

I uninstalled the Game. Is my data gone?

Yes, everything the Game kept on that device is removed. Data already sent to Google and Unity follows section 4.

Does the Game have an account I need to close?

No. The Game has no accounts and no sign-in, so there is no account data to delete on any server.
